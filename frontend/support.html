Frequently Asked Questions
How can I improve my GPA? ▼
Focus on your weaker modules first — use the GPA Calculator to identify which subjects drag your average down the most. Attend tutorials, download lecture notes from the Student Material section, and review past papers. Consistent study habits and early exam preparation make the biggest difference. You can also join study groups via the Forum.
How to upload lecture notes? ▼
Go to Student Material → select the correct tab (PPT, Lecture Notes or Tutorial) → scroll down to the Upload Zone at the bottom of that section → click "Choose File to Upload". Supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX. Files are reviewed by moderators before publishing. You'll get a notification once approved.
Where can I download study materials? ▼
All materials are under Student Material in the sidebar. Click the PPT, Lecture Notes, or Tutorial tab to filter by type. Each card has a Download button. You can also find resources on the Home page under Latest Lecture Notes, Quick Revision Notes, and Recently Added Resources.
How is GPA calculated at RUSL? ▼
GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credits. Each grade maps to a grade point (A/A+ = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, etc.). For each module, multiply grade points × credits. Sum all results to get Total Grade Points, then divide by the total number of credits. Use our GPA Calculator to do this automatically.
How do I find materials for my department? ▼
Visit Modules in the sidebar to browse by department. Each department card (ITM, ACF, MGT, HRM, MKT, THM) shows degree type and credits. Materials are tagged with department codes — you can quickly identify ITM, ACF, HRM etc. from the coloured badges on each resource card.
